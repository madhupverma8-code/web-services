ℹ️ Important: Additional Costs (Paid by Client)

The prices mentioned above are strictly for Website Design & Development. The following costs are not included and must be paid directly by the client:

  • Domain Name: Approx. ₹800 - ₹1,200 / year.
  • Web Hosting: Approx. ₹2,000 - ₹4,000 / year.
  • Database & Server: Approx. ₹500 - ₹1,000 / month for high traffic (if required).
  • SMS OTP (DLT Registration): One-time DLT fee ₹5,000 - ₹6,000 + ₹0.15 - ₹0.25 per SMS.
    How to do DLT Registration?
    1. The client must register on the telecom operator's DLT portal (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL).
    2. Upload company documents (GST, PAN, Aadhaar) and sign an authorization letter.
    3. Verify via OTP on the registered mobile number.
    4. Pay the registration fee directly to the operator.
    5. Once approved, share the DLT credentials with us to send OTPs.
  • WhatsApp OTP: ₹0 - ₹2,000 (Setup) + ₹0.12 - ₹0.18 per message. (No DLT needed).
  • Email Verification: Free up to 100-300 emails/day. Paid plans start from ₹800 for 10,000 emails.
  • Payment Gateway (e.g., Razorpay): ₹300 one-time registration fee + approx. 2% transaction charges + 18% GST on the fee.
    How to register for Razorpay?
    1. Visit razorpay.com and create a new account.
    2. Select "Individual" or "Company" based on your business type.
    3. Upload required documents (PAN Card, GST Certificate, Bank Statement/Cancelled Cheque).
    4. Verify your email and mobile number via OTP.
    5. Pay the one-time setup fee (₹300) to activate your account.
    6. Share the API keys with us to integrate it into your website.
